---
name: Article discovery intent
description: Editorial intent behind article discovery on SofoServis marketing pages.
---

Article discovery should support useful advice and topical connections, not imply guaranteed rankings or compete with the enquiry flow. Do not broaden it into an identical unrelated feed in the global footer.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritizes protecting enquiries and showing genuinely relevant advice over adding links everywhere for assumed SEO benefit.

**How to apply:** When extending discovery to additional surfaces, preserve the quote-form priority and select advice for its relevance to that surface rather than filling empty slots.
